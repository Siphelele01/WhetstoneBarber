Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026. These terms apply to every booking made with Whetstone Barber Co., whether online, by phone or in person.

Bookings

Online bookings are held for the barber, date and time you select. We recommend arriving five minutes early — appointments booked back-to-back mean a late arrival may shorten your service time.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Please give us at least four hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule, by phone or email, so we can offer the slot to another customer. Repeated no-shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

Payment

Payment is taken in-shop after your service, by card or cash. No payment is required to make an online booking.

Age & guardianship

Customers under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the appointment.

Liability

We take reasonable care with every service. Please tell your barber before your appointment about any skin conditions, allergies or sensitivities so we can adjust products or technique accordingly.

Your information

Details you provide when booking (name, email, phone) are used only to confirm and manage your appointment, and are not sold or shared with third parties.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to book@whetstonebarber.co.za or by calling 021 555 0142.
