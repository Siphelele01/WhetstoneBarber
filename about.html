About Whetstone

Named after the stone, not the steel.

Why the name

A whetstone doesn't cut anything itself. It's what a barber uses, quietly, before the work even starts — to keep a blade honest. We opened in 2018 on that idea: the visible cut is only as good as the unglamorous preparation behind it.

That's shaped how we run the shop. We don't rush consultations, we don't share clippers between guards without a clean, and we train specifically on the cut most people actually want — a proper skin fade, blended by hand rather than guessed at.

We're three barbers on the floor, a waiting bench that's usually empty because we book properly, and a shop that smells like bay rum instead of anything synthetic.
